See it

Two phones, one beat

Drift happens. Duet fixes it quietly.

Phones run at slightly different speeds and networks add delay. Duet measures the gap continuously and nudges each player back into line without you ever hearing a jump.

  • Clock offsets measured like NTP, trusting only the fastest replies
  • Small drift eased out by playing a touch faster or slower
  • Bluetooth delay lined up with a per-headphone nudge
  • If a phone falls far behind, it lands back on the beat

Under the hood

How it works

01

Start a session

Duet gives you six easy words and a QR code. Read them out on a call, or send the link.

02

Everyone joins

Phones find each other directly — over the internet, or on the same Wi-Fi with no internet at all.

03

Press play

The host sends each song ahead of time, and every phone plays it at the same moment and stays there.

Privacy

Your music goes phone to phone.

There’s no Duet account and no Duet server. Songs travel directly between your phones, encrypted, and are deleted when the session ends.
